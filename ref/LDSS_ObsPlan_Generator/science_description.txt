This is a photometric and spectroscopic follow-up program of stripped-envelope and strongly-interacting supernovae from the Young Supernova Experiment, a time-domain survey which combines proprietary Pan-STARRS photometry with public data from ZTF and ATLAS to achieve the highest cadence of any ongoing transient survey. We will select young and rapidly-rising supernovae using our combined photometric monitoring, and then obtain coincident LDSS-3 imaging and spectra for classification.  Following classification, we continue to monitor the confirmed transients at late phases.  Our main science goals include characterizing late-time circumstellar interaction in core-collapse and stripped-envelope supernovae, and probing the spectral evolution of faint and rapidly-evolving transients, to better understand the diversity of stellar death and pre-explosion mass loss. Our Magellan nights are used to follow our classified events once they become too faint for smaller telescopes, as well as to obtain classifications of new objects.
